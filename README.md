# CashTrack - Automated SMS Expense & Revenue Tracker

## Features
- **Real-time Bank/Card/UPI SMS Parser**: Automatically detects and saves debits and credits in the background.
- **6-Month Historical Import**: Imports the last 180 days of financial SMS on first launch.
- **Manual Add & Edit**: Tap `+ Add Entry` for cash transactions, or tap any existing SMS transaction to edit its category, amount, or delete it.
- **Monthly Analytics & Budget Alerts**: Category-wise pie chart and automatic alerts at 80% and 100% of your monthly budget.
- **CSV / Excel Export**: One-tap export of all transactions.
